
#  *chapter 1 - the shakedown*
*day 1* 
left private property in Melbourne in afternoon and drove to Primrose Park near Traralgon. we got bogged in a muddy spot upon arrival to campsite and failed to recover before sunlight. slept uneasy with the cats a bit unsettled in a windy, rainy, muddy environment near a dead tree. 

*day 2*
woke to the same sloppy conditions and spent hours trying to recover without success. called the local general store to get assistance but whilst waiting a local drove past and helped us out with a push. after rescue, we drove to Bunnings and purchased recovery gear and heavy duty tubs to house all of the muddy gear. we also purchased strong magnets to help hold the fabric up at night to block light. drove to 90 mile beach and stayed at Golden Beach camp 2. 

*day 3*
cats went on morning walk for approx. 90 minutes, Lochie stood on glass (non emergency - but clean up after yourself please campers). we realised we need a tent and walkie talkies to separate whilst outside with cats. 2 paralysis ticks found in Kami's fur which we removed and then treated both cats with tick preventative immediately. we drove to Bairnsdale to get a tent from fb marketplace second hand but it was the wrong size. drove to Bombala and stayed at Black Lake reserve.

*day 4*
woke up to hundreds of sheep and lamb around car. cats were very scared. drove to Fyshwick to get tent and then to private property in Canberra. set up tent and realised we needed more magnets to be contained. Lochie worked at bakery. close to midnight, Smudge left van through gap between tent and door as Lochie entered. tracked her with head torch - spotting her reflective eyes 100m away. used cat whispering powers to coax her back into van. 

*day 5*
drove to private property in Sydney to recover. 

*day 6*
drove to private property in Central Coast and found a paralysis tick feeding on Kami. removed and stressed heavily for the rest of the day.

*day 7*
had a picnic with family and stressed.

*day 8*
drove back to Sydney to recover. 

*day 9*
drove to Lake George but cats were acting really strange - running into bushes so we left. drove to Yass and stayed at Joe OConnor RV park. 

*day 10*
drove to private property in Melbourne and the wire door was locked with a secret bill inside that we didn't expect. Lochie had to break the lock and repair the door. 

# *chapter 2 - castlemaine*
*day 11*
drove to Castlemaine to explore the town as a potential base for the future. procured some nice food and herbs and drove to Leanganook camping ground. 

*day 12*
woke up and decided to stay another day here. cats were relaxing in front seats all day but in the afternoon the weather turned really stormy and windy and knocked over our setup. we packed up swiftly and drove back to private property in Melbourne.

# *chapter 3 - mia mia's revelation*
*day 13*
drove to Macedon, then Woodend to collect food. drove to Cobaw forest dam looking for a campsite but the weather was not friendly for forest sleeping (strong winds) so we left and drove to Mia Mia oval. here Lochie rested due to feeling unwell and struggling with allergies. we were woken by local cricketers coming to the oval which prompted us to leave and find another place to rest. drove to the Mia Mia dam for the night. 

*day 14*
after a sick and restless night accompanied by some anxious feelings about being disconnected from friends and family, Lochie decided to pull the pin on this trip which was supposed to go out to the Grampians, down to Port Fairy and back along the Otways. returned to private property in Melbourne to recover. 

# *chapter 4 - launch*
*day 15*
left late in the afternoon towards Eildon but were delayed and failed to launch - staying the night in private property in Melbourne. 

*day 16*
left early towards Eildon, stopping at Yarck bakery and planning where to stay for the night. drove to Doctor's Creek reserve and enjoyed the beautiful ferny creek campsite. 

*day 17*
moved the car in the morning out of position due to fear of being bogged. enjoyed another day at the reserve and planned the microscopy. some other campers gifted us some firewood which Lochie used to make a fire to cook some food. this was the day we also learned about the button man.

*day 18*
drove to Wangaratta and then Albury to collect some safety supplies that we realised we were missing in Doctor's Creek. drove to Ebden to stay at Ludlow's reserve. it was incredibly busy.

*day 19*
drove to Jingellic through the pine forest areas. we stayed at the reserve behind the local pub which had access to running water, including hot water for a fee. had another campfire here to test how the pine litter burns - it went great. amelia "enjoyed" a hot shower. cats really relaxed with this kind of travel now.

*day 20*
in the morning Lochie legitimately enjoyed a hot shower. we collected some samples from the surface of the earth to look at under the microscope and found some life in the topsoil which was really encouraging. the setup was both neck and back breaking - tripod necessary to hold camera. had a meal at the Jingellic pub to support local economy. 

*day 21*
left Jingellic and drove to Tumbarumba to get screw pegs, followed by Tumut for other supplies but we couldnt find them so we drove to Yass and stayed at Joe OConnor RV park. cricketers came here but we were not scared off this time. 

*day 22*
drove to Canberra to scour the tip for supplies without much luck other than a car mirror and a dovetail saw. enjoyed a laksa and spring rolls before driving to private property in Sydney. 

*day 23*
stayed in Sydney for the day - Lochie struggling with allergies so went to collect supplies to heal. 

*day 24*
drove to private property in Central Coast. 

*day 25*
drove to Blue Mountains via. Silverwater again looking for supplies for rooftop storage but didnt have any luck. stayed in Megalong Valley for the night which was very busy. 

*day 26*
drove to Lithgow and got excellent pie, sausage roll, coffee and cookie from the Tin Shed. drove to a couple of campsites on the way to Mudgee and then Cassilis but couldn't find a spot we felt safe at. we were shocked with the scale and number of mining sites near Sydney. ultimately we drove 7 hours this day and landed back in the Central Coast.
