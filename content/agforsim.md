this project initialises a vision to build a food forest on every farm in australia. 

agforsim begins as an honours thesis where i collect all code available on the internet related to modelling food forests, ecosystems, polycultures, etc. and make them into ensemble models that can be combined in novel ways to give the growers of the world more ability to cultivate with nature's patterns. 

i have also been collecting practice-first tools to use as grounding benchmarks for the theoretical tools. those with their hands in the soil wont find much use in models that don't reflect simple observations that they see every day. 

building from both angles allows me to connect practice to theory and theory to practice -  building a bridge for those sitting on either side to walk across and work together in the future.

at this point i am unsure what i will add to this page about this project, since i have a private vault for this while its being developed. i will try to post updates though for anyone who might be interested.
