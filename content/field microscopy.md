with a microscope on the road (gratefully borrowed from BioQuisitive) we can zoom in on all of the life around us that the naked eye cannot see. 

i was hoping to take a microscope around to dozens of WWOOF hosts, organic farms, natural forest areas and the likes to collect samples but as of this moment in November 2025 I have only collected from 4 sites

## site 1 -- jingellic
near the water was an old campfire site with a beautiful wattle within reach. i took a drop of water from the river as sample1. i mixed a drop of clean water into crushed litter as sample2, moved the litter aside and put some clean water on topsoil for sample3. next i moved to the campfire site and put clean water in the ash for sample4. i was interested to see some plant matter so i took a single blade of grass from around the fire and a single leaf from the wattle as sample5 and sample6. 

it took us a while to get the camera right. the microscope lens was a lot more difficult to work with than either of us anticipated and holding a camera steady at the same time proved to be quite the headache. the flies didnt make it any more enjoyable. once we did, we were surprised. no movement at all in any of the samples except sample3. 

the beauty of the shots are undeniable, with samples representing a wave battered shoreline. and to see a small worm of some sort under the microscope in sample3 was really amazing. we were pretty bored after looking at stills and were unsure if continuing to use the microscope would be worth the time. once we saw life, we were hooked.
